Frequently Asked Questions

How do I contact Konekta support during weekends? expand_more
Our platform support is available 24/7 through the Live Chat. For physical office visits in São Tomé, we operate Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM.
What should I do if a client cancels at the last minute? expand_more
Please refer to our Cancellation Policy section. Providers are eligible for a partial payout if the cancellation occurs within 2 hours of the scheduled service time.
How can I update my professional certifications? expand_more
Navigate to 'Service Settings' in your sidebar, then select 'Credentials'. You can upload photos of your new certificates there for verification.
